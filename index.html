Public affairs · Data communication · Lab 05

Good analysis needs a clear path to its audience. This site explores how a small, well-organized website can make methods and supporting material easier to find.

Built from the Beautiful Jekyll template, this project brings together three practical skills: organizing content with Markdown, applying styles to specific page elements, and generating a table from structured data. Each example keeps the underlying files available for inspection.

Why this matters

For public-facing research, accessibility includes more than making a document available. Readers also need a coherent structure and enough context to understand what they are seeing. A reusable publishing workflow can support that goal while making revisions easier to trace.
